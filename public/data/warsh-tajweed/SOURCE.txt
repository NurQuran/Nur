The color-span files are derived for use inside Nūr. A span is shown only when
the displayed Warsh verse exactly matches the Quranpedia text in its file.
Differences between the Quranpedia and QUD texts are projected only across
identical Unicode codepoints; changed characters never receive a rule color.
